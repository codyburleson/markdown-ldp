X supports Y — X provides evidence or argument in favour of Y.

The epistemic layer (`spec/02` §5.8). Schema.org is web-publishing shaped and
has no word for this; CiTO does. It matters more than it looks: a vault-local
`supports` would have nothing to align *up to*, leaving the most
reasoning-relevant predicates outside the hierarchy exactly where recall
matters most.
